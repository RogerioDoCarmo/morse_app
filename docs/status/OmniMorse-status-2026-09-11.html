The videos, in detail

This is the one unfinished deliverable, and the only one where the machinery is complete but the output is not. It is worth reading before the next attempt.

Three runs reported success and produced unusable footage. continue-on-error on the emulator step is what preserves partial footage when a flow falls over — and it also swallows five flows failing in a row. Verification is looking at a frame, not at the run's conclusion. Nothing else caught any of the three failures below.
RunSymptomCause, and the fix
1no clips at allandroid-emulator-runner splits its script: on newlines and runs each line through its own sh -c, so a shell function was torn apart mid-brace. The logic moved to a script file.
2“Pixel Launcher isn’t responding” across every frameA pixel_6 profile at 1080p through swiftshader, with animations on for the whole job, ANRs the launcher; the dialog then hid the app from Maestro. Error dialogs are suppressed, and animations moved from the workflow to the recorder — boot cheap, record properly.
3the wrong screen in three of four cellswaitForAnimationToEnd: timeout: N does not wait for N — N is a maximum, and returns in under a second on a screen that is not animating. The dwell now comes from the recorder, which runs a fixed length and is never stopped early.

Two design decisions that came out of it

  • Both outputs trim from the END of a clip, never the front. Everything variable — boot, Maestro’s driver, the app launch, flow retries — happens at the front; what is worth watching is at the end. A fixed front trim was wrong by about fifty seconds the moment flows retried.
  • Playback drops to 5 WPM before signalling. A message at the default speed is over in about three seconds, which is why the play assertion failed — and a cell that plays for three seconds of sixteen is a still image for the rest. Duration is bought with speed rather than with filler characters.

When a run finally produces good footage

  • Re-framing costs seconds of ffmpeg, not another run: re-compose from the store-videos-raw-clips artifact.
  • Play takes a YouTube URL for the promo video, not an upload — and there are five ways to paste one it rejects: extra parameters, private, age-restricted, monetised, or no feature graphic for it to sit on.
  • There is no sound and there cannot be. screenrecord captures video only. Music belongs on the YouTube upload.
