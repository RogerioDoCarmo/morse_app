OmniMorse

Encode. Decode. Learn.

A Morse code translator for iOS and Android. Type or speak a message and send it as sound, light, screen flashes or vibration — or tap it back into text.

App Store Privacy Support Repository

What it does

  • Typed text and speech become Morse, drawn as dots and dashes on screen
  • Plays a message as sound, on the torch, as screen flashes, or as vibration
  • Decodes by tap input — press duration tells a dot from a dash, with a cut-off you set yourself
  • Reads the decoded text aloud
  • Learn and Tips pages, in English, Portuguese and Spanish

Android

OmniMorse is on Google Play in closed testing and is not yet publicly listed, so there is no Play link here to follow. If you would like to join the Android test, write to contact@rogeriodocarmo.com.

Privacy

Your settings stay on your device and nothing is sent but optional crash diagnostics, which you can switch off. Speech recognition is performed by the operating system, which may send audio to Apple or Google to transcribe — the app never stores it. The full policy is at privacy-policy.html.

Support

Something not working, or an idea for it? support.html has the contact details and what to include so a problem can actually be reproduced.
