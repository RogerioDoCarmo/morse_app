What Morse code is
A way of sending text as two signal lengths — a short one and a long one. Morse and Vail worked it out in the 1830s for the electric telegraph, and it is still readable by ear, by lamp, or by a single finger on a key.
THE SILENCE COUNTS TOO
Everything is measured in one unit — the length of a dot. A dash is three. The gaps tell letters and words apart.
